 Arizona Complete Health-Complete Care Plan Dual Eligible 
Member Preferred Drug List  
AzCH-CCP covers select drugs for AHCCCS recipients who are eligible for Medicare. These include drugs that are:  
• 
Over-the-counter (OTC)    
• 
Excluded from coverage under Medicare Part D 
Drugs must also be safe and cost effective.  This is not a complete list of all covered drugs.   
If you need a drug that is not on the drug list, your doctor may request prior authorization at https://www.covermymeds.com/epa/envolverx/  or 
your doctor may fax a prior authorization form located at https://www.azcompletehealth.com/providers/pharmacy.html
