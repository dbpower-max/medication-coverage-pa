Pima & Yuma County Crisis Medication List  
The Arizona Complete Health-Complete Care Plan Crisis Medication list includes medications 
that offer clinical safety and efficacy in a behavioral health crisis. This list of medications is for 
use in adults or children that are Non-title XIX/XXI and Non-SMI who present in crisis at the 
CRC Level 1 Subacute Behavioral Health Facility in PIMA County OR HHW Community 
Observation Unit in YUMA County. 
The medications listed will help stabilize an individual in crisis and bridge them to a follow-up 
outpatient appointment. Medication quantity limits are noted. One additional 7 day supply of 
prescribed medication will be allowed.
