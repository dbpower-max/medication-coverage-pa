  1.2.2 Patient meets at least TWO of the following:  
• 
Platelet count of less than 20,000/mcL  
• 
Absolute neutrophil count of less than 500/mcL  
• 
Absolute reticulocyte count of less than 60,000/mcL  
 
AND 
 
2 - Prescriber attests that other known causes of bone marrow failure (BMF) have been ruled 
out (e.g., immune thrombocytopenia [ITP], hodgkin lymphoma or non-hodgkin lymphoma, 
aplastic anemia associated with paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria [PNH])  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is 6 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
4 - Prescribed by a physician with expertise in the treatment of severe aplastic anemia in a 
center with expertise in hematopoietic stem cell transplants with shared decision-making 
regarding the potential risks of treatment with Omisirge  
 
AND 
 
5 - Provider attests that the patient has never received Omisirge treatment in their lifetime
