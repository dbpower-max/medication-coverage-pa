dexamethasone, hydrocortisone, methylprednisolone) for adrenal insufficiency as one of the 
following:  
 
 3.1 Both of the following:  
• 
Patient is 4 to 17 years old  
• 
Daily GC dose is greater than 12 mg/m2/day in hydrocortisone dose equivalents  
 
OR 
 
 3.2 Both of the following:  
• 
Patient is 18 years of age or older  
• 
Daily GC dose is greater than 13 mg/m2/day in hydrocortisone dose equivalents  
 
AND 
 
4 - Prescribed by an endocrinologist
