1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Brand Provigil, generic modafinil, Brand Nuvigil, generic armodafinil  
Diagnosis 
Narcolepsy, Obstructive Sleep Apnea, Shift Work Disorder, Idiopathic 
Hypersomnia (off label)  
Approval Length 
12 month(s)  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - ONE of the following diagnoses:  
• 
Narcolepsy  
• 
Excessive sleepiness due to obstructive sleep apnea

• 
Excessive sleepiness due to shift work disorder (circadian rhythm sleep disorder, shift 
work type)  
• 
Idiopathic hypersomnia  
 
AND 
 
2 - If the request is for Brand Provigil or Brand Nuvigil, the patient has a history of failure, 
contraindication, or intolerance to its preferred generic counterpart (i.e., generic modafinil or 
generic armodafinil)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is 18 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
4 - ONE of the following:  
 
 4.1 Patient is NOT taking concomitant stimulant medication (e.g., methylphenidate, 
amphetamine-dextroamphetamine, lisdexamfetamine)  
 
OR 
 
 4.2 BOTH of the following:  
 
  4.2.1 Patient is currently taking concomitant stimulant medication (e.g., methylphenidate, 
amphetamine-dextroamphetamine, lisdexamfetamine)  
 
AND 
 
  4.2.2 Prescribed by or in consultation with ONE of the following:  
• 
Psychiatrist  
• 
Neurologist  
• 
Pulmonologist specializing in sleep medicine
