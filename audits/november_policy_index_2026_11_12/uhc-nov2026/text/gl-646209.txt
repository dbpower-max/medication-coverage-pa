AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming patient has mutation(s) in the 
collagen type VII alpha 1 chain (COL7A1) gene  
 
AND 
 
3 - Medication is being used for the treatment of wounds  
 
AND 
 
4 - Medication will be applied by a healthcare professional  
 
AND 
 
5 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming wound(s) being treated meet 
ALL of the following criteria:  
• 
Adequate granulation tissue  
• 
Excellent vascularization  
• 
No evidence of active wound infection in the wound being treated  
• 
No evidence or history of squamous cell carcinoma in the wound being treated  
 
AND 
 
6 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a dermatologist  
 
AND 
 
7 - Medication is NOT being used concurrently with other FDA approved therapies (e.g., 
Filsuvez) for the treatment of epidermolysis bullosa
