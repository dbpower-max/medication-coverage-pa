OR 
 
 1.2 Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus  
 
AND 
 
2 - Patient is 6 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is unable to self-inject medications (e.g. Humalog, Lantus, Levemir) due to ONE of 
the following:  
• 
Physical impairment  
• 
Visual impairment  
• 
Lipohypertrophy  
• 
Documented needle-phobia to the degree that the patient has previously refused any 
injectable therapy or medical procedure (refer to DSM-5 for specific phobia diagnostic 
criteria)  
 
AND 
 
4 - Forced Expiratory Volume (FEV1) within the last 60 days is greater than or equal to 70% 
of expected normal as determined by the physician  
 
AND 
 
5 - Afrezza will not be approved in patients with ONE of the following:  
• 
Who smoke cigarettes  
• 
Who recently quit smoking (within the past 6 months)  
• 
With chronic lung disease (e.g. asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease)
