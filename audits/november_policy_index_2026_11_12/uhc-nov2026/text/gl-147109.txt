Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming a diagnosis of eosinophilic 
esophagitis (EoE)

AND 
 
2 - Patient has symptoms of esophageal dysfunction (e.g., dysphagia, food impaction, 
heartburn, abdominal pain)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient has at least 15 intraepithelial eosinophils per high power field (HPF)  
 
AND 
 
4 - Other causes of esophageal eosinophilia have been excluded  
 
AND 
 
5 - Patient is 11 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
6 - Paid claims or submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming trial and failure 
(of a minimum 8-week duration), contraindication, or intolerance to a proton pump inhibitor 
(e.g., pantoprazole, omeprazole)  
 
AND 
 
7 - Paid claims or submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming trial and failure 
(of a minimum 8-week duration), or intolerance to a topical (esophageal) corticosteroid (e.g., 
budesonide, fluticasone)  
 
AND 
 
8 - Prescribed by or in consultation with ONE of the following:
