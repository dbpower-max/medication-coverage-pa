Guideline Name 
Non-Solid Oral Dosage Forms  
Formulary 
• 
Medicaid - Community & State Arizona  
 
 
Guideline Note: 
Effective Date: 
6/1/2026  
 
Note: 
The purpose of this guideline is to target high-cost oral solution formulations that are duplicative 
in clinical nature. For oral solutions without preferred alternatives available, patient may receive 
the requested drug if they demonstrate inability to swallow. For oral solutions with preferred 
alternatives that only exist in solid oral dosage formulations (e.g., capsules, tablets), patients will 
be asked to try the preferred solid oral dosage formulation unless they have an inability to 
swallow. Not all oral solutions that fit the criteria below will be added to this PA guideline. 
Certain oral solutions may have drug-specific PA criteria applied if the drug-specific PA criteria 
can provide more tailored and/or robust clinical review.

Approval Criteria    
 
1 - ONE of the following:  
 
 1.1 Requested drug is FDA (Food and Drug Administration)-approved for the condition being 
treated  
 
OR 
 
 1.2 If requested for an off-label indication, the off-label guideline approval criteria have been 
met  
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or verification of paid claims confirming 
ONE of the following:  
 
 2.1 Trial and failure, or intolerance to generic lamotrigine tablet or lamotrigine ODT  
 
OR 
 
 2.2 Patient is unable to swallow a solid dosage form (e.g., oral tablet, capsule) due to ONE of 
the following:  
• 
Age  
• 
Physical impairment (e.g., difficulties with motor or oral coordination)  
• 
Dysphagia  
• 
Patient is using a feeding tube or nasal gastric tube  
 
OR 
 
 2.3 For continuation of prior therapy defined as no more than a 45-day gap in therapy
