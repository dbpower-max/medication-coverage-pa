1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Ryoncil  
Approval Length 
1 month(s)  
Therapy Stage 
Initial Authorization  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis of acute graft 
versus host disease (aGvHD)

AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming ONE of the following:  
 
 2.1 Patient has Grade C or D aGvHD involving the skin, liver, and/or GI tract  
 
OR 
 
 2.2 Patient has Grade B aGvHD involving the liver and/or GI tract with or without concomitant 
skin disease  
 
AND 
 
3 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming disease is steroid refractory 
as defined by progression of aGvHD within 3 days or no improvement within 7 days of 
consecutive treatment with 2 mg/kg/day methylprednisolone or equivalent  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient is 2 months of age to less than or equal to 17 years of age  
 
AND 
 
5 - Patient has NOT received any second line therapy to treat aGvHD (e.g., Jakafi, 
mycophenolate mofetil, etc.)  
 
AND 
 
6 - BOTH of the following:  
 
 6.1 Prescribed by a provider at a transplant center with expertise in gene therapy  
 
AND
