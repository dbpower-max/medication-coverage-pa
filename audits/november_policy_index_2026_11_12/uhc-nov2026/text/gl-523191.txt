Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming the mutation or deletion of 
genes in chromosome 5q resulting in ONE of the following:  
 
 1.1 Homozygous gene deletion or mutation of SMN1 gene (e.g., homozygous deletion of 
exon 7 at locus 5q13)

OR 
 
 1.2 Compound heterozygous mutation of SMN1 gene [e.g., deletion of Survival of Motor 
Neuron 1 (SMN1) exon 7 (allele 1) and mutation of SMN1 (allele 2)]  
 
AND 
 
2 - ONE of the following:  
 
 2.1 BOTH of the following:  
 
  2.1.1 Diagnosis of symptomatic spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) confirmed by a neurologist 
with expertise in the diagnosis and treatment of SMA  
 
AND 
 
  2.1.2 Patient is less than 2 years of age  
 
OR 
 
 2.2 ALL of the following:  
 
  2.2.1 Diagnosis of SMA based on the results of SMA newborn screening  
 
AND 
 
  2.2.2 Patient has 4 copies or less of Survival of Motor Neuron 2 (SMN 2)  
 
AND 
 
  2.2.3 Patient is less than or equal to 6 months of age  
 
AND

3 - Patient is NOT dependent on invasive ventilation  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient is NOT dependent on the use of non-invasive ventilation beyond use for naps and 
nighttime sleep  
 
AND 
 
5 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting patient's anti-AAV9 
antibody titers are less than or equal to 1:50  
 
AND 
 
6 - BOTH of the following:  
 
 6.1 Provider attests that the patient has never received onasemnogene abeparvovec 
treatment in their lifetime  
 
AND 
 
 6.2 Provider attests that the patient is NOT receiving concomitant chronic survival motor 
neuron (SMN) modifying therapy for the treatment of SMA (e.g., Spinraza, Evrysdi)  
 
AND 
 
7 - Prescribed by a neurologist with expertise in the diagnosis of SMA

1 - BOTH of the following:  
 
 1.1 Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis of spinal muscular 
atrophy (SMA)  
 
AND 
 
 1.2 Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming the presence of a SMN1 
gene mutation  
 
AND 
 
2 - Patient is 2 years of age or older and under 17 years old  
 
AND 
 
3 - BOTH of the following:  
 
 3.1 Provider attests that the patient has never received onasemnogene abeparvovec (e.g., 
Zolgensma) treatment in their lifetime  
 
AND 
 
 3.2 Provider attests that the patient is NOT receiving concomitant chronic survival motor 
neuron (SMN) modifying therapy for the treatment of SMA (e.g., Spinraza, Evrysdi)  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient will commence systemic corticosteroids equivalent to oral prednisolone at 1 mg per 
kg of body weight per day (mg/kg/day), starting one day prior to Itvisma therapy and planned 
for a total of 30 days  
 
AND 
 
5 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming the absence of hepatic 
dysfunction [e.g., alanine aminotransferase (ALT), total bilirubin, gamma-glutamyl transferase

(GGT) or glutamate dehydrogenase (GLDH) less than or equal to the upper limit of normal 
(ULN)]  
 
AND 
 
6 - Provider attests that patient has NOT had clinical signs or symptoms of infection within 30 
days prior to treatment  
 
AND 
 
7 - BOTH of the following:  
 
 7.1 Patient can sit independently  
 
AND 
 
 7.2 Patient has never had the ability to walk independently  
 
AND 
 
8 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming anti-AAV9 antibody titers are 
less than or equal to 1:50  
 
AND 
 
9 - Patient’s clinical signs and symptoms onset occurred at greater than or equal to 6 months 
of age  
 
AND 
 
10 - Patient is NOT dependent on any of the following:  
• 
Invasive ventilation  
• 
Awake noninvasive ventilation for greater than 6 hours during a 24-hour period  
• 
Noninvasive ventilation for greater than 12 hours during a 24-hour period
