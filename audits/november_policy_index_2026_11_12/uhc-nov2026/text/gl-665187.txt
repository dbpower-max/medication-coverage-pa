Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting ALL of the following:  
 
 1.1 Diagnosis of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD)  
 
AND

 1.2 Mutation in the DMD gene has been confirmed  
 
AND 
 
 1.3 No deletion in exon 8 or exon 9 in the DMD gene is present  
 
AND 
 
 1.4 Patient is 4 through 5 years of age (at least 4 years 0 days and less than 6 years old)  
 
AND 
 
 1.5 Results of baseline North Star Ambulatory Assessment (NSAA) confirming the patient is 
ambulatory without needing an assistive device (e.g., without side-by-side assist, cane, 
walker, wheelchair) as evidenced by NSAA scoring  
 
AND 
 
 1.6 Anti-AAVrh74 total binding antibody titers are less than 1:400  
 
AND 
 
 1.7 Patient has been on a stable dose of a corticosteroid, unless contraindicated or 
intolerance, prior to start of therapy and will be used concomitantly with a corticosteroid 
regimen pre- and post- infusion  
 
AND 
 
 1.8 Results of ALL of the following laboratory values in the 30 days prior to therapy:  
• 
Liver function (i.e., clinical exam, GGT, total bilirubin)  
• 
Platelet counts  
• 
Troponin-I

AND 
 
 1.9 Patient has a left ventricular ejection fraction of greater than or equal to 40 percent (%)  
 
AND 
 
 1.10 Patient does NOT have clinical signs or symptoms of infection  
 
AND 
 
 1.11 A one-time gene therapy for ambulatory children ages 4 through 5 years of age for the 
treatment of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy. Patient will NOT receive Duvyzat (givinostat) or 
exon-skipping therapies for DMD [e.g., Amondys (casimersen), Exondys 51 (eteplirsen), 
Viltepso (viltolarsen), Vyondys 53 (golodirsen)] concomitantly or following Elevidys treatment  
 
AND 
 
 1.12 Patient has never received Elevidys treatment in their lifetime  
 
AND 
 
2 - Provider attests that patient does NOT have preexisting liver impairment, chronic hepatic 
condition, or acute liver disease  
 
AND 
 
3 - Provider attests that liver function (clinical exam, GGT, and total bilirubin) will be monitored 
weekly for the first 3 months following Elevidys infusion and monitoring will be continued 
thereafter if clinically indicated as per FDA (Food and Drug Administration)-approved 
recommendations in the labeling  
 
AND 
 
4 - Provider attests that these laboratory values will be monitored after administration 
according to the FDA-approved recommendations in the labeling:
