1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Ycanth  
Approval Length 
12 Week(s)  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Diagnosis of molluscum contagiosum  
 
AND 
 
2 - Patient is 2 years of age or older  


--- PDF page 1131 ---
UnitedHealthcare Community Plan of Arizona – Clinical Pharmacy Guidelines 
Page 1131 
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient has single or multiple, 2- to 5-mm-diameter, flesh-colored to translucent, dome-
shaped papules, some with central umbilication  
 
AND 
 
4 - ONE of the following:  
• 
Patient has eczema (e.g., atopic dermatitis)  
• 
Patient is immunocompromised  
• 
Patient has extensive involvement or experiences bleeds, secondary infections or 
discomfort from the lesions  
 
AND 
 
5 - Lesions have NOT resolved within six months of diagnosis  
 
AND 
 
6 - Trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance to ALL of the following:  
• 
imiquimod  
• 
topical salicylic acid  
• 
topical tretinoin  
• 
podofilox (e.g., Condylox gel, podofilox solution)  
• 
cimetidine  
• 
Tea Tree oil preparations  
 
AND 
 
7 - ONE of the following:  
 
 7.1 Patient is treating new lesions that have NOT previously been treated with Ycanth  
 
OR

 7.2 Lesions have previously been treated with Ycanth and will NOT exceed a total of 4

Zelsuvmi) on the same lesion for the treatment of molluscum contagiosum  
 
AND 
 
9 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a dermatologist
