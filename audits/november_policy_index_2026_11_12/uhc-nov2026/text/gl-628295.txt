AND 
 
2 - Medication is being used for hyperphagia due to PWS  
 
AND 
 
3 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting disease is confirmed by the 
presence of a mutation in chromosome 15 as detected by an FDA (Food and Drug 
Administration)-approved test or a test performed at a facility approved by Clinical Laboratory 
Improvement Amendments (CLIA)  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient is 4 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
5 - Patient does not have hepatic impairment or renal impairment  
 
AND 
 
6 - ALL of the following:  
• 
Baseline body weight or BMI (body mass index) has been obtained  
• 
Fasting plasma glucose and HbA1c (hemoglobin A1c) have been evaluated prior to 
initiating treatment  
• 
For patients with hyperglycemia, prescriber confirms blood glucose has been 
optimized prior to initiating treatment  
• 
Patient's blood glucose and HbA1c will be monitored periodically during treatment  
 
AND 
 
7 - Patient is able to successfully swallow the number of tablets necessary to achieve the 
target maintenance dose
