Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting a diagnosis of aromatic L-
amino acid decarboxylase (AADC) deficiency  
 
AND

2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis by ONE of the 
following:  
 
 2.1 Presence of mutations in the DDC (DOPA decarboxylase) gene as detected by an FDA 
(Food and Drug Administration)-approved test or a test performed at a facility approved by 
Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA)  
 
OR 
 
 2.2 CSF (cerebrospinal fluid) analysis showing a pattern of ALL of the following:  
• 
Reduced levels of 5-hydroxyindoleacetic acid (5-HIAA), homovanillic acid (HVA), and 
3-methoxy-4-hydroxyphenylglycol (MHPG)  
• 
High concentrations of 3-O-methyldopa (3-OMD), L-Dopa, and 5-OH-tryptophan (5-
HTP)  
• 
Normal pterins (e.g., neopterin, biopterin)  
 
OR 
 
 2.3 Analysis of plasma showing reduced aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase (AADC) 
activity  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient presents with the clinical signs and symptoms of the disease (e.g., hypotonia, 
oculogyric crises, developmental delays)  
 
AND 
 
4 - Assess skull maturity prior to the administration of Kebilidi  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by a provider in a medical treatment center with expertise in gene therapy  
 
AND

6 - Prescribed by ONE of the following:  
•
