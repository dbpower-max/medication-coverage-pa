Guideline Type 
Quantity Limit  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - ONE of the following:  
 
 1.1 For insulin dependent or pregnant patients, the physician must confirm the patient 
requires a greater quantity because of more frequent blood glucose testing (e.g., patients on 
intravenous insulin infusions)  
 
OR 
 
 1.2 For non-insulin dependent patients, ONE the following:  
 
  1.2.1 The patient is experiencing or is prone to hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia and requires 
additional testing to achieve glycemic control  
 
OR 
 
  1.2.2 The patient’s physician is adjusting medications and the patient requires additional 
blood glucose testing during this time  
 
OR
