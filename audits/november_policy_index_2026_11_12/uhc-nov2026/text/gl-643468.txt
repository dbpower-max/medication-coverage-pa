Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Diagnosis of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia (PSVT)  
 
AND 
 
2 - Patient has a history of sustained PSVT symptoms lasting at least 20 minutes despite 
performing vagal maneuver  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient has a history of emergency care (e.g. emergency room, urgent care) for PSVT 
conversion  
 
AND 
 
4 - ONE of the following:  
• 
Patient is not a candidate for ablation  
• 
Ablation is planned  
• 
Patient has a history of prior ablation  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by or in consultation with ONE of the following:  
• 
Cardiologist  
• 
Electrophysiologist  
• 
Emergency medicine physician
