Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Diagnosis of alkaptonuria (AKU)  
 
AND 
 
2 - Used for the reduction of urine homogentisic acid (HGA)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Disease is confirmed by ONE of the following:  
• 
Presence of a mutation in the homogentisate 1,2-dioxygenase (HGD) gene  
• 
Presence of elevated homogentisic acid (HGA) levels in urine greater than 0.4 g/24h  
 
AND 
 
4 - Trial (of a minimum 30-day supply) or intolerance to generic nitisinone 2 mg capsules  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by or in consultation with ONE of the following:  
• 
Rheumatologist  
• 
Geneticist  
• 
Specialist experienced in the treatment of inborn errors of metabolism
