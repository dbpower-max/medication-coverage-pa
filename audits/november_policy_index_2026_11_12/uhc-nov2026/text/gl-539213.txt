Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming BOTH of the following:  
• 
Diagnosis of Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD)

• 
Disease is confirmed by the presence of a mutation of the dystrophin gene amenable 
to exon 51 skipping  
 
AND 
 
2 - Patient is 7 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
3 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a neurologist with expertise in the treatment of DMD  
 
AND 
 
4 - Dose will not exceed 30 milligrams per kilogram of body weight once weekly  
 
AND 
 
5 - Submission of medical records including results of baseline North Star Ambulatory 
Assessment (NSAA) confirming the patient is ambulatory without needing an assistive device 
(e.g., without side-by-side assist, cane, walker, wheelchair) as evidenced by NSAA scoring  
 
AND 
 
6 - Patient will not receive Exondys 51 in combination with Duvyzat (givinostat) or other exon-
skipping therapies for DMD [e.g., Amondys 45 (casimersen), Viltepso (viltolarsen), Vyondys 
53 (golodirsen)]
