Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting a diagnosis of moderate-
to-severe thyroid eye disease (TED), confirmed by BOTH of the following:  
• 
Clinical Activity Score (CAS) greater than or equal to 4  
• 
Orbital imaging (CT or MRI) confirming extraocular muscle or orbital fat involvement
