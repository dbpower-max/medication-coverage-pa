2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes, lab results) documenting detectable HDV 
RNA within the 6 months before initiating therapy  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient has active liver disease (e.g., ALT above the upper limit of normal [ULN], evidence 
of hepatic fibrosis by noninvasive testing [e.g., transient elastography])  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient is without decompensated liver disease (e.g., Child-Pugh Class B or C)  
 
AND 
 
5 - ONE of the following:  
• 
Patient is receiving concomitant therapy for hepatitis B virus (HBV) (e.g., entecavir, 
tenofovir disoproxil fumarate)  
• 
Patient is NOT a candidate for HBV therapy  
 
AND 
 
6 - Prescriber attests that the patient has been counseled on the importance of adherence to 
therapy with risk of disease reactivation and need for ongoing monitoring if discontinued  
 
AND 
 
7 - Prescribed by or in consultation with ONE of the following:  
• 
Hepatologist  
• 
Gastroenterologist  
• 
Infectious disease specialist
