2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or laboratory results confirming the 
presence of at least one mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator 
(CFTR) gene  
 
AND 
 
3 - ONE of the following:  
 
 3.1 CFTR mutation is responsive based on clinical or in vitro data  
 
OR 
 
 3.2 CFTR mutation results in the production of CFTR protein  
 
AND 
 
4 - ONE of the following:  
 
 4.1 For granules packets, patient is at least 2 to less than 6 years of age  
 
OR 
 
 4.2 For tablets, patient is 6 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by, or in consultation with, a specialist affiliated with a CF care center
