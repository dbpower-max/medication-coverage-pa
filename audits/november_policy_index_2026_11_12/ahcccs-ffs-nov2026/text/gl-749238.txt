 1.2 Patient has thrombocytosis (i.e., platelets greater than or equal to 450 x 10^9/L)  
 
AND 
 
 1.3 One of the following:  
 
  1.3.1 Presence of a JAK2, calreticulin (CALR), or myeloproliferative leukemia virus 
oncogene (MPL) mutation  
 
OR 
 
  1.3.2 Presence of other clonal markers (e.g., presence of a TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A, SF3B1, 
EZH2, IDH1, IDH2, DNMT3A, SRSF2, U2AF1, SF3B1, TP53, IKZF1, NF-E2, or CUX1 
mutation)  
 
AND 
 
 1.4 No evidence of reactive (secondary) thrombocytosis (e.g., thrombocytosis due to 
infection, inflammation, or iron deficiency anemia)  
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or verification of paid claims confirming 
trial and inadequate response, contraindication, or intolerance to hydroxyurea  
 
AND 
 
3 - Prescribed by or in consultation with one of the following:  
• 
Hematologist  
• 
Oncologist
