Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Diagnosis of retinopathy of prematurity (ROP)  
 
AND 
 
2 - ONE of the following:  
• 
Patient gestational age at birth less than or equal to 32 weeks  
• 
Patient birth weight less than or equal to 1500 grams  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient weight greater than 800 grams on day of treatment  
 
AND 
 
4 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting retinopathy of prematurity 
(ROP) is present in at least one eye with one of the following classifications:  
• 
ROP zone 1, stage 1 plus, 2 plus, 3, or 3 plus  
• 
ROP zone 2, stage 2 plus or 3 plus  
• 
AP - ROP (aggressive posterior ROP)  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by or in consultation with an ophthalmologist experienced in the treatment of 
retinal diseases  
Notes  
*NOTE: Provider must Buy and Bill, and submit a medical claim for rei
mbursement.
