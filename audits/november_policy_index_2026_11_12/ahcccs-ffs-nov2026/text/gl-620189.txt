AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis by one of the 
following:  
• 
Enzyme assay demonstrating deficient iduronate 2 sulfatase (I2S) activity  
• 
Presence of iduronate-2-sulfatase (IDS) gene mutation as detected by a FDA-
approved test or a test performed at a facility approved by Clinical Laboratory 
Improvement Amendments (CLIA)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Disease is one of the following:  
• 
Presymptomatic  
• 
Symptomatic  
 
AND 
 
4 - Requested medication is initiated prior to advanced neurologic impairment (e.g., 
progressive cognitive decline, status epilepticus)  
 
AND 
 
5 - Patient's weight is greater than or equal to 5 kg  
 
AND 
 
6 - Prescribed by or in consultation with one of the following:  
• 
Geneticist  
• 
Metabolic disease specialist  
• 
Physician who specializes in the treatment of lysosomal storage disorders  
 
AND
