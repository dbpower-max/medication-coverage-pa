Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming a diagnosis of recurrent

AND

2 - Patient has had, or is scheduled to have, surgical debulking of visible laryngotracheal 
papillomas associated with the current recurrence to establish minimal residual disease 
before the first dose of Papzimeos (Note: Historical debulking procedures associated with 
prior disease episodes do not satisfy this requirement)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Provider attests that patient has never received a treatment course with Papzimeos in their 
lifetime  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient is 18 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a specialist knowledgeable in the treatment of 
recurrent respiratory papillomatosis (e.g., otolaryngologist, pulmonologist, oncologist)
