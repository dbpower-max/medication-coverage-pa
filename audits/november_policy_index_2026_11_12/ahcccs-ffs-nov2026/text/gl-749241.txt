Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Diagnosis of obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM)  
 
AND 


--- PDF page 296 ---
Page 296 
 
2 - Patient has New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class II or III symptoms (e.g., shortness 
of breath, chest pain)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming both of the following were 
measured prior to Camzyos therapy:  
 
 3.1 Patient had a left ventricular ejection fraction of greater than or equal to 55%  
 
AND 
 
 3.2 Patient had a valsalva left ventricular outflow tract (LVOT) peak gradient greater than or 
equal to 50 mmHg at rest or with provocation  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient weighs greater than or equal to 30kg  
 
AND 
 
5 - Trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance to both of the following at a maximally 
tolerated dose:  
• 
non-vasodilating beta blocker (e.g., bisoprolol, propranolol)  
• 
calcium channel blocker (e.g., verapamil, diltiazem)  
 
AND 
 
6 - Requested drug will not be used in combination with another cardiac myosin inhibitor (e.g., 
Myqorzo)  
 
AND 
 
7 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a cardiologist
