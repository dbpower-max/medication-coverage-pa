Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting a diagnosis of hereditary

AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming patient has a transthyretin 
(TTR) mutation (e.g., V30M)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming one of the following:  
• 
Patient has a baseline familial amyloidotic polyneuropathy (FAP) stage of 1 or 2  
• 
Patient has a baseline neuropathy impairment score (NIS) greater than or equal to 10 
and less than or equal to 130  
• 
Patient has a baseline Karnofsky Performance Status score greater than 50%  
 
AND 
 
4 - Presence of clinical signs and symptoms of the disease (e.g., neuropathy, quality of life)  
 
AND 
 
5 - Patient has not had a liver transplant  
 
AND 
 
6 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a neurologist
