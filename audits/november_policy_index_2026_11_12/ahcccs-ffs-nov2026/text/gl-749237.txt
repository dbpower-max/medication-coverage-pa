Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming ALL of the following:  
 
 1.1 Diagnosis of warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia (wAIHA)  
 
AND 
 
 1.2 wAIHA has been present for at least 3 months  
 
AND 
 
 1.3 Patient has evidence of hemolysis (e.g., elevated lactate dehydrogenase (LDH), bilirubin 
level above the normal reference range, increased reticulocyte count, decreased haptoglobin 
levels)  
 
AND 
 
 1.4 Patient has a hemoglobin level less than or equal to 10.0 gram per deciliter (g/dL) prior to 
treatment initiation  
 
AND 
 
 1.5 Patient has a positive monospecific direct antiglobulin test (DAT)  
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or verification of paid claims confirming 
patient is currently receiving or has previously received treatment with a corticosteroid (e.g., 
prednisone)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is 12 years of age or older
