1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Tzield  
Diagnosis 
Stage 2 type 1 Diabetes Mellitus  
Approval Length 
1 authorization per lifetime  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis of stage 2 type 1 
diabetes mellitus  


--- PDF page 1888 ---
Page 1888 
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis by BOTH of the 
following:  
 
 2.1 Two or more of the following pancreatic islet autoantibodies  
• 
Glutamic acid decarboxylase 65 (GAD) autoantibodies  
• 
Insulin autoantibody (IAA)  
• 
Insulinoma-associated antigen 2 autoantibody (IA-2A)  
• 
Zinc transporter 8 autoantibody (ZnT8A)  
• 
Islet cell autoantibody (ICA)  
 
AND 
 
 2.2 Dysglycemia on oral glucose tolerance testing at baseline defined as one of the following:  
• 
Fasting plasma glucose greater than or equal to 100mg/dL and less than 126mg/dL 
(5.6 - 6.9 mmol/L)  
• 
2-hour plasma glucose greater than or equal to 140mg/dL and less than or equal to 
199mg/dL (7.8 -11.0 mmol/L)  
• 
Hemoglobin A1C 5.7 to 6.4% (39-47mmol/mol) or greater than or equal to 10% 
increase in A1C  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is 1 year of age or older  
 
AND 
 
4 - Presence of type 2 diabetes mellitus or other forms of diabetes (e.g., genetic forms of 
diabetes, maturity-onset diabetes of the young (MODY), latent autoimmune diabetes in adults 
(LADA), or diabetes secondary to medications or surgery) has been ruled out  
 
AND 
 
5 - Provider attests that patient does not have laboratory or clinical evidence of active Epstein-

Barr virus (EBV) and cytomegalovirus (CMV) infection (e.g., undetectable viral load by PCR 
testing) prior to initiating treatment with Tzield  
 
AND 
 
6 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) AND absence of paid claims confirming 
patient has never received Tzield treatment in their lifetime  
 
AND 
 
7 - Treatment will not exceed the recommended 14 doses  
 
AND 
 
8 - Prescribed by or in consultation with an endocrinologist

 once daily for 14 consecutive days.

Stage 3 Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus  
Approval Length 
12 month(s)  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis of stage 3 type 1 
diabetes mellitus  
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming diagnosis by BOTH of the 
following:  
 
 2.1 One of the following:  


--- PDF page 1890 ---
Page 1890 
• 
Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) ≥ 126 mg/dL (≥7.0 mmol/L)  
• 
2-Hour Plasma Glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L) during an oral glucose tolerance 
test  
• 
Hemoglobin A1C ≥ 6.5% (≥ 48 mmol/mol)  
• 
Random Plasma Glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L) in a patient with classic 
symptoms of hyperglycemia (e.g., polyuria, polydipsia, unexplained weight loss)  
 
AND 
 
 2.2 Presence of at least one positive pancreatic islet cell autoantibody (e.g., antibodies 
against glutamic acid decarboxylase, zinc transporter 8, insulin, islet cells, or islet antigen)  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is between 8 and 17 years of age  
 
AND 
 
4 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming evidence of residual beta-cell 
function as demonstrated by peak C-peptide ≥0.2 pmol/mL on a mixed-meal tolerance test (if 
a mixed meal tolerance test is not available, an alternative method for measuring peak C-
peptide ≥0.2 pmol/mL may be appropriate)  
 
AND 
 
5 - Provider attests that patient does not have laboratory or clinical evidence of active Epstein-
Barr virus (EBV) and cytomegalovirus (CMV) infection (e.g., undetectable viral load by PCR 
testing) prior to initiating treatment with Tzield  
 
AND 
 
6 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) AND absence of paid claims confirming 
patient has never received Tzield treatment in their lifetime  
 
AND

7 - Provider attests to initiate Tzield treatment as soon as possible following stage 3 type 1 
diabetes mellitus diagnosis but no later than 8 weeks from diagnosis  
 
AND 
 
8 - Treatment will not exceed two,12-day, treatment courses

9 - Prescribed by or in consultation with an endocrinologist
