Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting all of the following:  
 
 1.1 Diagnosis of late-onset Pompe disease (lysosomal acid alpha-glucosidase [GAA] 
deficiency)  


--- PDF page 1323 ---
Page 1323 
 
AND 
 
 1.2 Disease is confirmed by one of the following:  
• 
Absence or deficiency (less than 40% of the lab specific normal mean) of GAA 
enzyme activity in lymphocytes, fibroblasts, or muscle tissues as confirmed by an 
enzymatic assay  
• 
Molecular genetic testing confirms mutations in the GAA gene  
 
AND 
 
 1.3 Presence of clinical signs and symptoms of the disease (e.g., respiratory distress, 
skeletal muscle weakness, etc.)  
 
AND 
 
 1.4 Medication is used in combination with Pombiliti (cipaglucosidase alfa-atga)  
 
AND 
 
 1.5 Patient weight is greater than or equal to 40 kg  
 
AND 
 
2 - Opfolda is not substituted with other miglustat products (i.e., Zavesca, Yargesa)
