1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Preferred Drugs: Mounjaro, Trulicity, Brand Victoza, generic liraglutide  
Approval Length 
12 month(s)  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Both of the following:  
 
 1.1 Submission of medical records (e.g. chart notes, lab values) confirming both of the 
following:  
• 
Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus  


--- PDF page 719 ---
Page 719 
• 
Baseline A1C greater than or equal to 6.5% (lab report MUST be submitted)  
 
AND 
 
 1.2 Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or verification of paid claims 
documenting one of the following:  
• 
Verification of paid claims documenting history of failure to metformin at a minimum 
dose of 1500 milligrams (mg) daily for 90 days (Note: MUST be verified via paid 
claims)  
• 
Patient has contraindication or intolerance to metformin  
 
AND 
 
2 - Patient is 10 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
3 - Drug is not being used for weight loss  
 
AND 
 
4 - Provider attests that the requested medication will not be used concurrently with a DPP-4 
inhibitor-containing agent [e.g., Januvia (sitagliptin), Jentadueto (linagliptin-metformin), 
Tradjenta (linagliptin)] or a plan has been developed to address concomitant use  
 
AND 
 
5 - Medication is not being co-administered with another GLP-1 receptor agonist [e.g., 
Mounjaro (tirzepatide), semaglutide, Trulicity (dulaglutide), Victoza (liraglutide)]  
Notes  
If requested medication is being used to treat appetite suppression/we
ight loss or improve conditions caused by obesity (i.e., Zepbound for 
OSA) – deny the case for Plan Exclusion. For all other indications den
y the case for medical necessity and do not review for off-label use.
