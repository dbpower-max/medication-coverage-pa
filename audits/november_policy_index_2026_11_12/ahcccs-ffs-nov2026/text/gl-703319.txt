1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Duvyzat  
Approval Length 
6 month(s)  
Therapy Stage 
Initial Authorization  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming a diagnosis of Duchenne 
muscular dystrophy (DMD)  


--- PDF page 505 ---
Page 505 
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting one of the following:  
 
 2.1 Patient has a confirmed mutation of the dystrophin gene  
 
OR 
 
 2.2 Muscle biopsy confirmed an absence of dystrophin protein  
 
AND 
 
3 - Patient is 6 years of age or older  
 
AND 
 
4 - Submission of medical records including results of baseline North Star Ambulatory 
Assessment (NSAA) confirming patient is ambulatory without needing an assistive device 
(e.g., without side-by-side assist, cane, walker, wheelchair, etc.) as evidenced by NSAA 
scoring prior to initiating Duvyzat  
 
AND 
 
5 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) documenting BOTH of the following:  
• 
Baseline platelet count ≥ 150 x 10^9/L  
• 
Triglyceride level drawn prior to therapy initiation (document baseline TG level)  
 
AND 
 
6 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or paid claims confirming requested drug 
will be used concomitantly with a corticosteroid regimen (e.g., prednisone/prednisolone, 
Emflaza [deflazacort], Agamree)  


--- PDF page 506 ---
Page 506 
 
AND 
 
7 - Patient will not receive Duvyzat in combination with or following Elevidys (delandistrogene 
moxeparvovec-rokl) treatment or exon-skipping therapies for DMD [e.g., Amondys 
(casimersen), Exondys 51 (eteplirsen), Viltepso (viltolarsen), Vyondys 53 (golodirsen)]  
 
AND 
 
8 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a pediatric neurologist with expertise in treating DMD  
 
Product Name:Duvyzat  
Approval Length 
12 month(s)  
Therapy Stage 
Reauthorization  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming patient has experienced a 
benefit from therapy (e.g., improvement in preservation of muscle strength)  
 
AND 
 
2 - Submission of medical records including results of recent North Star Ambulatory 
Assessment (NSAA) confirming patient is maintaining ambulatory status without needing an 
assistive device (e.g., without side-by-side assist, cane, walker, wheelchair, etc.) as 
evidenced by NSAA scoring  
 
AND 
 
3 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or paid claims confirming patient 
continues to receive concomitant corticosteroid regimen (e.g., prednisone/prednisolone, 
Emflaza [deflazacort], Agamree)
