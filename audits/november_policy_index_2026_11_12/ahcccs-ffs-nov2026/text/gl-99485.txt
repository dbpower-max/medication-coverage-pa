AND

2 - Patient is premenopausal  
 
AND 
 
3 - History of trial and failure (e.g., inadequate pain relief), contraindication or intolerance after 
a three month trial of TWO analgesics (e.g., ibuprofen, meloxicam, naproxen)  
 
AND 
 
4 - History of trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance after a three month trial to ONE 
of the following:  
• 
Hormonal contraceptives  
• 
Progestins [e.g., norethindrone (generic Aygestin)]  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by or in consultation with ONE of the following:  
• 
Obstetrics/Gynecologist (OB/GYN)  
• 
Reproductive endocrinologist
