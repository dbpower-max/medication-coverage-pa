Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming a diagnosis of moderately to 
severely active ulcerative colitis (UC)

AND 
 
2 - One of the following:  
• 
Greater than 6 stools per day  
• 
Frequent blood in the stools  
• 
Frequent urgency  
• 
Presence of ulcers  
• 
Abnormal lab values (e.g., hemoglobin, ESR, CRP)  
• 
Dependent on, or refractory to, corticosteroids  
 
AND 
 
3 - Paid claims or submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming a trial and 
failure, contraindication, or intolerance to one of the following conventional therapies:  
• 
6-mercaptopurine  
• 
Aminosalicylate (e.g., mesalamine, olsalazine, sulfasalazine)  
• 
Azathioprine  
• 
Corticosteroids (e.g., prednisone)  
 
AND 
 
4 - Paid claims or submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming history of 
failure, contraindication, or intolerance to ALL of the following:  
• 
A preferred adalimumab biosimilar  
• 
infliximab  
• 
Xeljanz (tofacitinib) oral tablet (IR or XR)  
• 
A preferred ustekinumab biosimilar  
 
AND 
 
5 - Prescribed by or in consultation with a gastroenterologist
