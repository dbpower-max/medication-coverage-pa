1 .  Criteria 
 
Product Name:Praluent  
Diagnosis 
Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD) and Prevention of 
Cardiovascular Events in patients at increased risk for a major 
adverse cardiovascular (CV) event  
Approval Length 
12 month(s)  
Therapy Stage 
Initial Authorization  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Patient is 18 years of age or older  


--- PDF page 1393 ---
Page 1393 
 
AND 
 
2 - One of the following diagnoses:  
 
 2.1 Diagnosis of Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD) defined as acute coronary 
syndrome, history of myocardial infarction (MI), stable or unstable angina, coronary or arterial 
revascularization (e.g., percutaneous coronary intervention or coronary artery bypass graft 
surgery), stroke, transient ischemic attack, and/or peripheral arterial disease presumed to be 
of atherosclerotic origin  
 
OR 
 
 2.2 Patient is at increased risk for a major adverse cardiovascular (CV) event  
 
AND 
 
3 - Prescribed by, or in consultation with, a cardiologist, endocrinologist, or lipid specialist  
 
AND 
 
4 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or verification of paid claims confirming 
ONE of the following:  
 
 4.1 Patient has had an inadequate response to a high-intensity statin therapy as documented 
by ALL of the following:  
• 
Inadequate response with a high-intensity statin (i.e., atorvastatin 40-80 mg or 
rosuvastatin 20-40 mg) at the maximally tolerated dose for at least three consecutive 
months  
• 
Patient must be adherent to high-intensity statin, as evidenced by consistent 
pharmacy claims over the past three months  
• 
Statin therapy will be continued with Praluent therapy  
 
OR 
 
 4.2 Patient has had an inadequate response to a moderate-intensity statin therapy as 
documented by ALL of the following:  


--- PDF page 1394 ---
Page 1394 
• 
Patient has a documented intolerance or contraindication to at least one high-intensity 
statin therapy  
• 
Inadequate response with a moderate-intensity statin (i.e., atorvastatin 10-20 mg, 
rosuvastatin 5-10 mg, simvastatin 20-40 mg, pravastatin 40-80 mg, lovastatin 40 mg, 
fluvastatin XL 80 mg, fluvastatin 40 mg twice daily, or pitavastatin 2-4 mg) at the 
maximally tolerated dose for at least three consecutive months  
• 
Patient must be adherent to moderate-intensity statin, as evidenced by consistent 
pharmacy claims over the past three months  
• 
Statin therapy will be continued with Praluent therapy  
 
OR 
 
 4.3 Patient has had an inadequate response to a low-intensity statin therapy as documented 
by ALL of the following:  
• 
Patient has a documented intolerance or contraindication to at least one high-intensity 
and at least one moderate-intensity statin therapy  
• 
Inadequate response with a low-intensity statin (i.e., simvastatin 10 mg, pravastatin 
10-20 mg, lovastatin 20 mg, fluvastatin 20-40 mg, pitavastatin 1 mg) at the maximally 
tolerated dose for at least three consecutive months  
• 
Patient must be adherent to low-intensity statin, as evidenced by consistent pharmacy 
claims over the past three months  
• 
Statin therapy will be continued with Praluent therapy  
 
OR 
 
 4.4 Patient is statin intolerant (inability to tolerate any dose or increase the dose above the 
smallest tablet strength) or has a contraindication to all statins as documented by ONE of the 
following:  
• 
Patient experienced statin-related rhabdomyolysis with CK elevations greater than or 
equal to 10 times the upper limit of normal (ULN)  
• 
Patient experienced intolerable skeletal-related muscle symptoms (e.g., myopathy or 
myalgia) to at least two different statins at the lowest dose (one of the statin trials must 
include pravastatin or rosuvastatin) and the skeletal-related muscle symptoms (e.g., 
myopathy or myalgia) resolved upon discontinuation of each respective therapy  
• 
Patient has a labeled contraindication to all statins  
 
AND 
 
5 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) or verification of paid claims confirming 
ONE of the following:  


--- PDF page 1395 ---
Page 1395 
 
 5.1 Both of the following:  
• 
Patient had an inadequate response with maximally tolerated statin plus ezetimibe (or 
ezetimibe therapy alone if statin intolerant) for at least three consecutive months  
• 
Patient must be adherent to maximally tolerated statin plus ezetimibe therapy (or 
ezetimibe therapy alone, if statin intolerant), as evidenced by consistent pharmacy 
claims over the past three months  
 
OR 
 
 5.2 Patient has a documented intolerance or contraindication to ezetimibe  
 
AND 
 
6 - Medication will be used as adjunct to low-fat diet and exercise  
 
AND 
 
7 - Patient's documented current LDL-C is greater than or equal to 55 mg/dL (within the last 
30 days)  
 
AND 
 
8 - Praluent (alirocumab) will not be used in combination with another PCSK9 inhibitor (such 
as Repatha) or Juxtapid
