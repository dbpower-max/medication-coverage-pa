1 .  Criteria

6 month(s)  
Therapy Stage 
Initial Authorization  
Guideline Type 
Prior Authorization  
 
 
Approval Criteria    
 
1 - Submission of medical records (e.g., chart notes) confirming a diagnosis of cholestatic 
pruritus associated with primary biliary cholangitis (PBC)  


--- PDF page 1141 ---
Page 1141 
 
AND 
 
2 - PBC is confirmed by both of the following:  
• 
Antimitochondrial antibodies (AMA) or other PBC‐specific autoantibodies (e.g., sp100 
or gp210) as detected by serologic blood test  
• 
Biochemical evidence of cholestasis (e.g., elevated alkaline phosphatase (ALP), 
elevated gamma-glutamyl transferase (GGT), mildly elevated alanine 
aminotransferase (ALT), mildly elevated aspartate aminotransferase (AST))  
 
AND 
 
3 - Presence of moderate to severe itch  
 
AND 
 
4 - Patient is receiving concurrent therapy for PBC (e.g., Ursodiol, Iqirvo, Livdelzi), unless 
there is a contraindication or intolerance to these medications  
 
AND 
 
5 - Trial and failure, contraindication or intolerance to at least two of the following treatments 
from different mechanisms of action used for the relief of pruritus:  
• 
Antihistamines (e.g., diphenhydramine, hydroxyzine)  
• 
Rifampin  
• 
Bile acid sequestrants (i.e., cholestyramine, colestipol)  
• 
Opioid antagonists (e.g., naltrexone)  
 
AND 
 
6 - Prescribed by or in consultation with one of the following:  
• 
Hepatologist  
• 
Gastroenterologist
